# Design Recommendations

## Primary Recommendation

The baseline support plate is stiff under the studied mechanical loads. No geometry change is required for the current preliminary screening load set.

However, the stress plots show that the highest local stresses occur near mounting-hole, counterbore, rib and fillet transitions. If this component were developed further, the first design improvement should be local stress reduction around those regions.

Recommended geometry improvements:

- Increase fillet radius at rib-to-boss transitions.
- Increase underside mounting-hole fillet radius where practical.
- Smooth abrupt counterbore-to-plate transitions.
- Consider local reinforcement or washer-seat thickening if bolt-joint realism is added later.

## Why This Is The Right Next Design Move

The current results are not governed by global bending or gross deformation. They are governed by local stress concentration near support/load-transfer details. Improving those transitions would reduce peak stress without materially changing the overall stiffness or mass of the component.

## Future Design Variant

A useful next design iteration would compare:

| Design | Change | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | Current geometry | Local stress peaks near hole/fillet transitions |
| Improved | Larger fillets and smoother rib/boss transitions | Similar deformation with lower local peak stress |

This is recommended as future work rather than part of the frozen model, because the current project checkpoint is now focused on final documentation and reporting.
